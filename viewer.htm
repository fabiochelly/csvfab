Duplicates

Rows are duplicates when any key matches; within a key, every field must match once cleaned. A key whose fields are not all filled is ignored for that row. Groups chain: rows sharing an e-mail with one and a phone with another are one group. No field at all: the whole row is compared.
Delete keeps, in each group
